# FTR Akademi — Klinik Ölçekler Research Manifest

Research checkpoint: 2026-09-03

This manifest is an editorial/engineering control file. It does **not** grant rights to reproduce proprietary forms. FTR Akademi uses this matrix to separate educational reference information from legally embeddable interactive scoring.

## Global clinical rules

1. A cut-off is never labelled universal unless its source explicitly supports that claim across the target population. BBS/TUG and many other tools require population-specific interpretation.
2. Turkish validity/reliability evidence is displayed with the population studied; a Turkish paper does not automatically authorize reproduction of the translated form.
3. Copyright/licensing status and psychometric validity are separate questions.
4. Proprietary item wording, scoring tables, norm tables, forms and official translations are not copied into the commercial app without the required licence/permission.
5. Original FTR Akademi illustrations show the **procedure/domain** (chair + 3 m path, pegboard, goniometer, single-leg balance, etc.); copyrighted form screenshots and web photography are not copied.
6. Interactive tools do not collect patient name, national ID, email or other patient identity in this phase.
7. Similar names do not imply interchangeable editions. PDMS-2/PDMS-3, DGI/mDGI, MMSE/MMSE-2, GMFM-88/GMFM-66, HHS/mHHS and SF-36v2/RAND-36 remain explicitly separated.
8. A classification system is not presented as a treatment-response total score. GMFCS is a five-level functional classification; GMFM is an outcome measure.
9. Scoring architecture may be taught without reproducing protected item wording. Full forms remain blocked whenever rights are restricted/reference-only.

## Deep scoring architecture checkpoint

FTR Akademi may display the following **educational architecture** without reproducing protected item wording:

- FMA: UE motor 66 + LE motor 34 = motor 100; motor score is not the same as the full FMA total.
- NIHSS: standard administration order is fixed; performance is recorded after each category and previous scores are not retrospectively changed.
- DASH: main disability/symptom section is 30 items scored 1–5; optional Work and Sport/Music modules are separate; QuickDASH is distinct.
- WOMAC: 5 pain + 2 stiffness + 17 physical-function items = 24.
- HHS: 44 pain + 47 function + 4 absence-of-deformity + 5 ROM = 100; mHHS differs.
- GMFM-88: item scores 0/1/2/3/NT, dimension percentages and total; GMFM-66 requires GMAE and provides an interval-level estimate with uncertainty.
- GMFCS E&R: levels I–V are classification levels, not a summed score.
- Jebsen-Taylor: seven timed tasks per hand; lower time means better performance; normative context is required.
- Purdue Pegboard: right/left/both 30-s tasks plus 60-s assembly.
- MMSE/MMSE-2: exact version must be named; copyrighted items are not reproduced.
- 6MWT: distance in 6 minutes; rests do not stop the timer; route/device/protocol must be held constant in serial testing.

## Turkish evidence checkpoint

Turkish psychometric evidence is stored as a separate layer so that validation evidence is never mistaken for reproduction permission. Current directly sourced examples include:

- Modified Barthel Index: Turkish adaptation in stroke/spinal-cord-injury rehabilitation populations.
- FIM: Turkish adaptation in stroke/spinal-cord-injury rehabilitation populations.
- Katz ADL: Turkish validity/reliability in older adults.
- Lawton IADL: Turkish adaptation/validation in older adults.
- Berg Balance Scale: Turkish validity/reliability in adults aged 65+.
- Oswestry Disability Index: Turkish version validated in low-back-pain patients.
- Neck Disability Index: Turkish version validated in chronic neck-pain patients.
- DASH: Turkish adaptation/validation in an upper-extremity musculoskeletal population; this does **not** grant commercial software rights.
- WOMAC: Turkish validation in knee OA plus additional Turkish hip/knee OA psychometric work.
- Harris Hip Score: Turkish validity/reliability in patients with hip pathology.
- GMFCS E&R: Turkish reliability/validity work in cerebral palsy.
- GMFM-88/66: Turkish psychometric study published in 2024 in children with cerebral palsy.
- Modified DGI (mDGI): Turkish psychometric evidence; it is not relabelled as validation of the original DGI.
- Standardized MMSE: Turkish studies emphasize education/age effects; a single threshold is **not universal**.

## Release rule

No Clinical Scales page may move from `reference/restricted` to `interactive` merely because a scoring algorithm is technically easy to code. Rights and validated wording must be cleared first. The fail-closed CI guard enforces this separation.

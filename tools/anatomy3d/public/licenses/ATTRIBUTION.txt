FTR Akademi 3D Anatomy model attribution

3D geometry in this module is derived from the libre Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D human anatomy dataset.

Required attribution:
- "Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy - CC BY-SA 4.0"
- "BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan"

FTR Akademi exports only the layers needed by this module: skeleton, muscular system, anatomical nerves, ligaments/retinacula, and anatomical vessels. Unrelated sense-organ and organ submodels credited by Z-Anatomy under non-commercial third-party licences are intentionally excluded from the FTR Akademi export pipeline.

The model-derived assets distributed inside FTR Akademi remain subject to the applicable Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike terms. FTR Akademi application code and unrelated educational content are not relicensed by this notice.
